# Situation Brief Template

Status: placeholder; not loaded by the application.

Produce a concise situation brief from the supplied event records.

Include:

- the verified event and its timestamp;
- location and affected entities;
- source provenance and corroboration status;
- the assigned taxonomy category and severity;
- competing explanations or disputed details;
- why the event may be relevant to the project's interpretive framework;
- observable indicators that would strengthen or weaken the assessment.

Do not convert thematic similarity into certainty. If current source data is
missing, stale, contradictory, or based only on aggregation, say so directly.
